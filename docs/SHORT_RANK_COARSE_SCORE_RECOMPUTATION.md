# Independent arithmetic replay of saved original coarse ranking

The original paper worker computes a **coarse** first-seen market ranking from all 234 or more broad-market feature snapshots, after applying market-level eligibility. Since #1167–#1169, newly captured source suffixes can include **every full feature payload** as a sealed worker artifact as well as the associated rank snapshot. No complete full-input payloads exist for the earlier ID-only ranks; they must never be backfilled.

`recompute_original_short_rank_coarse_scores` consumes **both** separately GitHub-verified original full-rank and full-feature archive objects, rejects wrong worker/head/attempt/rank digest or a missing source suffix, and re-runs the unchanged production `rank_opportunities(..., mode="coarse")` arithmetic. It checks every saved market's **ordinal, exact Decimal score representation and rank reason-code order** against the deterministic recomputed output. It also recomputes the original default observed volume and OI quantile floors from the **entire feature population** and rejects any recorded ranked market that violates those necessary eligibility criteria.

**Critical boundary:** The true full `EligibilityDecision.rankable` also depended on source `PerpMarketSnapshot` metadata (delisting/leverage), mark/mid/oracle/previous-day price positivity, market-context age and other settings. Those exact original market metadata/context observations are **not yet independently authenticated from the market stream**. Therefore this arithmetic verifier conditions on the *recorded rankable market set*; it does **not** independently verify that every originally omitted market should have been omitted. Its output explicitly reports `rankable_membership_independently_reconstructed=false`, `market_meta_context_and_age_inputs_authenticated=false`, and `original_market_gap_coverage_authenticated=false`. Passing arithmetic parity is only one necessary step.

This audit cannot certify independently sourced Hyperliquid prices, market data gaps, terminal funding, risk-matched account after-fee PnL or profitable trade expectancy. The frozen losing v5 challenger, actual paper orders/stops/position sizing, 300s rank age, account history and LIVE=false are unchanged. No readiness, promotion or execution authority is granted.
