## D. Three distinct, falsifiable participant mechanisms

These are different economic treatments, not new names for range reclaim,
relative-value/repricing, funding receipt, weekend patterns, static imbalance,
TWAP, liquidation, unlocks/airdrops or the already assessed venue-delisting thesis.
All three remain hypotheses. The historical winners show capturable moves can
exceed friction, while gross-negative losers motivate identifying information
before entry; neither group proves these proposed causes.

### 1. Collateral utility changes — selected

**WHO:** Financing holders, borrowers and balance-sheet managers. **WHY:** A change
in recognized collateral value changes an asset's financing service. **WHEN:** An
original official flat ordinary Portfolio Margin ratio changes materially.
**WHAT:** Product scope, before/after ratios, announced rollout start, immutable
first receipt and current native eligibility. **HOW:** LONG increases, SHORT
reductions, at a fixed pre-effective clock without a price/volume trigger.
**PROFIT:** Residual inventory repricing might survive public anticipation and
costs. **FAILURE:** Holders release spare collateral rather than buy, substitute
stable collateral, change unrelated positions, or public anticipation removes
remaining movement. Notice selection may itself respond to asset risk.

The free [official example](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/79f36518401b4187b46d52e87bb2c286)
contains ASTER/ETC50%→60%, rollout starting October16at06:00UTC, approximately30min.
It distinguishes financing capacity from maintenance-risk effects. Those old
rows are quarantined source examples, never test events. A10pp increase recognizes
$100more collateral per$1000holding at unchanged prices; it is not a trading-profit
or price-impact estimate. No forced sale or compulsory buyer is identified.

### 2. Expansion of short-selling access

**WHO:** Previously constrained pessimists and hedgers. **WHY:** A newly borrowable
asset permits borrowing then selling spot, broadening ways to express bearish
views. **WHEN:** Genuine first borrow eligibility for an already spot-traded asset.
**WHAT:** Original official notice, product/underlying identity and first received
availability clock; historical existing-borrow/product census. **HOW:** A separate
fixed delayed SHORT-or-NO_TRADE test, never first-candle timing. **PROFIT:** Residual
price adjustment as constrained views become tradable. **FAILURE:** Equivalent
access already exists, leveraged long demand dominates, or anticipation/costs
consume adjustment. A new contract alone is not first global shortability.
[Official borrow addition](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/96e0b5370c9a49fbb5d66613b0d7ddf1).
Free example access is demonstrated; complete vintages/cohort are not admitted.
This is distinct from the old loss-of-spot-access/delisting thesis.

### 3. End of subsidized BNB principal-inventory demand

**WHO:** Farmers who acquire/rent BNB to obtain a temporary reward subsidy.
**WHY:** The incremental holding service ends when that farming incentive ends.
**WHEN:** A verified campaign end with no overlapping BNB campaign supporting the
same service. **WHAT:** Original official farming calendars, all overlapping
programs, principal-release rules and native BNB prices/funding. **HOW:** A separate
fixed BNB SHORT after expiry, not sale of the distributed reward token. **PROFIT:**
Subsidy-specific holdings may unwind after the reward service ends. **FAILURE:**
Early redemption/anticipation, retained BNB, overlapping incentives or costs erase
it. No forced sale or quantity is implied.
[Official calendar](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/announcement/detail/d5810276f8c44fa7ade2b93746cd6a0a)
and [principal-release rules](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/94ed108ce89d44ab8602aa3c476dfb04)
are free. The FAQ permits early unlock and specifies return within90minutes;
this weakens any assumption of a synchronized compulsory sale. Complete program
coverage is not established, so this is not selected.
